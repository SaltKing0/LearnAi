50 · Mini-Project: Attention Visualization

Explain it to a 12-year-old You build exactly the picture you saw in Concept 03 — but yourself. A grid that shows: which word looks how strongly at which other word. So "read" becomes "made yourself".

What you need

  • Attention theory (03) — Q/K/V, softmax, temperature.
  • The demo (playground/attention-demo.html) as a template.
  • p5.js / Canvas — draw the heatmap.

The visualization (self-built)

Enter a sentence — the heatmap shows which word looks how strongly at which other word. The scores here really come from a self-attention computation (Q·K, then softmax over temperature), just with toy embeddings instead of learned ones. Try the pronoun sentence and see what "he" focuses on.
