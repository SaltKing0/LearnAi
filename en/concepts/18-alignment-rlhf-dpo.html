18 · Alignment (RLHF & DPO)

Explain it to a 12-year-old Imagine you have a dog. The dog barks at everyone passing by — that's the "base model" (just guessing text). Now you train the dog: a treat when it's friendly, "no" when it bites. Alignment is that training — the model is trained from "guessing text" to "being helpful".

Why alignment?

A base model (just pretrained) produces the most likely next word. It's not helpful, not safe, not honest. Alignment gets the model onto the right wavelength.

RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)

  1. Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) → the model learns from good examples
  2. Reward model → humans rate answers (good/bad)
  3. PPO optimization → the model is optimized for high reward

DPO (Direct Preference Optimization)

DPO is the modern alternative to RLHF. Instead of a separate reward model, the model learns directly from preferences (A is better than B). Simpler, more stable, less compute.

Constitutional AI (CAI)

Anthropic's approach: instead of human ratings there is a "constitution" (principles) the model follows itself. The model rates and corrects itself against these principles.

The alignment tax problem

Alignment makes models safer, but often also less capable. Too much alignment = a "yes-man" that says nothing. Too little alignment = dangerous. The sweet spot is hard to find.
