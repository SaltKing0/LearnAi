28 · Alignment (RLHF & DPO)

Explain it to a 12-year-old Imagine you have a dog. The dog barks at everyone who walks by — that's the "base model" (just guessing text). Now you train the dog: treat when friendly, "no" when biting. Alignment is this training — the model goes from "guessing text" to "being helpful".

Why Alignment?

A base model (just pretrained) makes the most likely next word. It's not helpful, not safe, not honest. Alignment brings the model to the right wavelength.

RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)

  1. Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) → Model learns from good examples
  2. Reward Model → Humans rate responses (good/bad)
  3. PPO Optimization → Model is optimized for high reward

DPO (Direct Preference Optimization)

DPO is the modern alternative to RLHF. Instead of a separate reward model, the model learns directly from preferences (A is better than B). Simpler, more stable, less compute.

Constitutional AI (CAI)

Anthropic's approach: Instead of human ratings, there's a "constitution" (principles) that the model follows itself. The model evaluates and corrects itself based on these principles.

The Alignment-Tax Problem

Alignment makes models safer, but often also less capable. Too much alignment = "yes-man" that says nothing. Too little alignment = dangerous. The sweet spot is hard to find.
