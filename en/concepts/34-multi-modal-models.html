34 · Multi-Modal Models

Explain it to a 12-year-old Imagine a model is like someone who can only read (text). Now give them eyes (images), ears (audio), and hands (video). Suddenly they can not only understand a sentence, but explain a meme, read a diagram, or show someone playing flute. That's multi-modality.

What does Multi-Modal mean?

Multi-modal = the model processes multiple input types (modalities): text, images, audio, video, code, sensor data. The future belongs to models that speak all these languages.
