00 · Getting Started: What is AI anyway?

Explain it to a 12-year-old AI is like a very clever parrot. He's heard so much that he can mimic almost everything — but he doesn't really understand what he's saying. Sometimes he talks nonsense, and you only notice when you ask.

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is an umbrella term for systems that do tasks that normally require human intelligence: understand language, recognize images, solve problems, make decisions.

What is a "model"?

A model is the result of training. You show the computer millions of examples (text, images), and it learns patterns. The result — the "learned" — is the model.

What is "training"?

Training is like practice for the computer. You give it an example, it guesses, you say if it was right, and it adjusts. It does this millions of times.

What is "inference"?

Inference is deployment. The trained model gets a new input and gives an output. Like a student after learning in the exam.
