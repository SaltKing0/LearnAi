43 · RAG vs. Fine-Tuning vs. Prompting

Explain it to a 12-year-old Three ways to help someone smart: prompting = explain everything needed inside the question (cheapest). RAG = put the right documents on their desk (for up-to-date knowledge). fine-tuning = train them for a long time until they know it cold (for fixed behavior). Rule of thumb: start cheap and only escalate when the cheap tier demonstrably isn't enough.

The order is no accident: cost & risk rise from top to bottom.

Why this is central for you as a code user

  • The most expensive trap: fine-tuning too early when a better prompt or RAG would have sufficed. Measure first (Concept 15), escalate second.
  • Not either/or: production systems often combine a fine-tune (behavior) + RAG (knowledge) + prompt (task).
  • Evaluate the tiers separately: retrieval quality (30) and answer quality are different metrics.
