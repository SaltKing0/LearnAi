06 · Inference vs. Training

Explain it to a 12-year-old Training is like studying for a test — you learn from books, make mistakes, try again. Inference is the test itself — you use what you learned to answer questions. Training takes days and expensive computers. Inference takes seconds and runs on your phone.

Training

Training is the process of teaching the model. It requires massive compute (thousands of GPUs), massive data (terabytes of text), and massive time (weeks to months). The result is a model file.

Inference

Inference is using the trained model. You give it input, it gives output. Much cheaper than training — can run on a laptop for small models, or a server for large ones.

Why this matters to you

  • API costs → You pay for inference, not training
  • Latency → Inference speed determines user experience
  • Fine-tuning → You can do "mini-training" on your data
