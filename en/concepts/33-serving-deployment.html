33 · Serving & Production Deployment

Explain it to a 12-year-old Imagine you have a chef (the model) who bakes fantastic pizza. But he bakes in your kitchen (your laptop) and only for you. Serving is like opening a restaurant: The chef works in the kitchen, but 100 people can order at the same time, and all get their pizza in 5 minutes. Serving makes the model scalable.

Why Serving is different from Inference

Single inference (one prompt → one answer) is simple. But what if 1000 users send a request simultaneously? What if a request consists of 10,000 tokens? What if the model needs to be reloaded without downtime?
