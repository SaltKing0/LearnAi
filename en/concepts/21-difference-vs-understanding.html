21 · Difference vs. Understanding

Explain it to a 12-year-old When you hear "apple", you think of something red, sweet, that you eat — you've eaten one. The model only knows the words that often appear near "apple". It has never tasted an apple. That is a big, important difference.

"Knowing" vs. "meaning"

The model stores statistical relationships between tokens in its weights (04/05). It "knows" that "Paris" often appears near "France" — but it has no experiential meaning. This separation (syntax/probability vs. semantics/experience) is the core of what AI doesn't have.

Why this matters practically

  • Robustness: real understanding doesn't collapse in new situations; pattern-guessing does (16).
  • Trust: an answer "sounds" confident but is guessed (01) — dangerous for decisions.
  • Creativity: the model recombines learned material, but "invents" nothing from its own drive.
