05 · Training Intuitively

Explain it to a 12-year-old Imagine you're learning to shoot baskets. You shoot, you miss, someone says "too far left", you adjust. After thousands of attempts, you hit almost every time. Training a model is the same — it makes guesses, gets feedback, adjusts. Millions of times.

The Training Process

  1. Forward Pass → Model makes a prediction
  2. Loss Calculation → How wrong was it?
  3. Backpropagation → Which weights caused the error?
  4. Weight Update → Adjust weights to reduce error

Key Concepts

  • Loss → A number representing "wrongness". Lower = better.
  • Learning Rate → How big are the adjustments? Too big = unstable, too small = slow.
  • Epoch → One full pass through the training data.
  • Batch Size → How many examples before an update?

Training Data

LLMs are trained on massive text corpora: books, websites, code, Wikipedia. The quality and diversity of this data determines what the model "knows".

Why this matters to you

  • Hallucinations → Bad training data = bad outputs
  • Bias → Training data biases show up in outputs
  • Fine-tuning → You can continue training on your own data
