46 · What Can't AI Do?

Explain it to a 12-year-old A model is like someone who has read endless books but has never lived a single day. It can talk about things wonderfully — but it has no real experiences, no feelings, no own will. Some things need exactly that.

Limits that are structural (not just "not yet")

  • No consciousness / no experience — it processes symbols, "knows" nothing about the world (Concept 01/16).
  • No real counting/logic at the core — guesses patterns, stumbles over the simplest math without tools (09).
  • No causality from its own insight — only correlates; "why" is derived, not grasped.
  • No responsibility — decisions need a human.
  • Doesn't learn from life — only from data someone feeds it.

"Not yet" vs. "in principle"

Some things (better math, longer context) are technically solvable. Others (consciousness, real understanding) concern the nature of the architecture (01–04) and are not a question of "more data". Distinguishing these is important — otherwise you overestimate the system.
