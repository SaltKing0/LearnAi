42 · Quantization & Compression

Explain it to a 12-year-old Imagine a picture in Super-HD (4K) — looks fantastic, but takes a whole hard drive. Quantization is like shrinking it to Full HD: You lose a little quality, but it runs on every tablet. For models: less memory, less compute, minimally less intelligence.

Why Quantization?

A 70B model needs ~140GB RAM in FP16 format. No normal person has that. Quantization reduces it to ~35GB (Q4) or even ~20GB (Q2) — and suddenly it runs on a gaming GPU.

Quantization Methods

  • PTQ (Post-Training Quantization) → Compress model after training. Simple, but quality loss.
  • QAT (Quantization-Aware Training) → Model learns compression during training. Better, but expensive.
  • GPTQ → Meta's method, layer by layer with Optimal Brain Surgeon.
  • AWQ → Compress important weights less than unimportant ones.
  • GGUF/GGML → Format for llama.cpp, runs on CPU.
