Memory Tiers

An agent's three memory levels: Core (in the prompt, small, always present), Recall (recent history + summaries), Archival (external knowledge, only reachable via search). Add facts and watch what lands in the prompt, what moves into the summary, and what gets banished to external storage.

What to watch for The core is small and can't hold everything — important recurring facts (preferences) stay there; one-off details move to Recall/Archival. “Put everything in context” never fits.
