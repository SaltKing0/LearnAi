Sorting & Big-O

Watch four sorting algorithms race on the same random array. Each bar is a value; red = being compared, green = already sorted. The comparison counter is your live proof of Big-O: bubble/insertion grow ~n², merge/quick ~n·log n.

What to watch for Double the array size (n) and re-sort: the O(n²) sorts take ~4× as many comparisons, the O(n log n) sorts only a bit more than 2×. That gap is the whole reason complexity notation exists (see Concept 01).
